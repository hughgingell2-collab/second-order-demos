    About this play

    The second-order idea this demo exists to show.

    Lens

    Market-making.

    First-order wedge

    A government legal panel management platform — spend, allocation and performance dashboards for agencies.

    What it accrues

    Matter-level flow data: what government sends out, to whom, at what rate, with what outcome — across agencies, not inside one.

    The two parties

    Agencies (buyers with reporting obligations) and panel firms (sellers who fly blind on why they win or lose).

    Endgame

    Agency-side SaaS first, firm-side analytics second, then the benchmarks reprice the panel market and re-tenders run through the platform.

    What would validate it

    One agency legal operations lead confirming allocation is done today by memory and spreadsheet, and that they would trust a tool to recommend rather than decide. Start with a state agency or council, not the Commonwealth.
