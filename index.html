Second-Order Plays — demo series

First-order ideas are "an AI tool that does X's job." The second-order question: when the tool works, what does it know that nobody else knows — and which two parties could that knowledge connect?

Six interactive product mockups of the endgame each first-order wedge is designed toward. All data is fictitious. Built August 2026.
